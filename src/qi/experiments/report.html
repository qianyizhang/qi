New here? A guide to reading this report
  1. Choose one visit budget and position to compare like-for-like samples.
  2. Read decision time and charged visits together. More search work does not guarantee a better move.
  3. Use paired-color outcomes for games and immediate wins for the proven tactical targets. A partial run has missing evidence.
  4. Replay a decision, then explore its search trace. Check score perspective and score bound before interpreting a value.
